Sample Project Information (Overview/Description)

Project Title  
Sample Project: Customer Support Knowledge Base Portal

Project Overview  
This project delivers a web-based knowledge base portal designed to help customers quickly find accurate answers to common questions, troubleshoot issues, and access product documentation without requiring direct support assistance. The portal centralizes support content, improves self-service success rates, and reduces repetitive ticket volume by providing searchable, well-organized, and consistently maintained information.

The system supports two primary audiences: end users who consume help content and internal teams who create, curate, and govern that content. End users benefit from intuitive navigation, robust search, and clear article formatting. Internal teams benefit from streamlined authoring tools, review workflows, analytics, and content lifecycle management.

Project Objectives  
- Provide a single, reliable source of truth for customer-facing help content.  
- Enable fast discovery of relevant information through structured navigation and powerful search.  
- Reduce inbound support requests by improving self-service capabilities.  
- Improve content quality and consistency via editorial standards and approval workflows.  
- Provide analytics to measure content performance and identify knowledge gaps.  
- Ensure secure access controls for internal content and role-based administration.

Scope and Key Features  
1) Content Management  
- Article creation with rich text formatting, images, code snippets, and attachments.  
- Categorization using topics, tags, and product areas to support browsing and filtering.  
- Versioning, revision history, and rollback to maintain content integrity.  
- Content lifecycle states (draft, in review, published, archived).

2) Search and Discovery  
- Full-text search across titles, body content, tags, and metadata.  
- Relevance ranking with support for synonyms and common misspellings.  
- Filters by product, category, content type, and last updated date.  
- “Suggested articles” and related content recommendations.

3) User Experience  
- Mobile-responsive design with accessible typography and navigation.  
- Clear article templates (how-to, troubleshooting, FAQ) for consistent reading experience.  
- Feedback mechanisms (helpful/unhelpful, comments or issue reporting) to improve content.  
- Localization-ready structure to support multiple languages when needed.

4) Governance and Workflow  
- Role-based access (reader, contributor, editor, administrator).  
- Approval workflow with assigned reviewers and publishing controls.  
- Editorial guidelines support (style rules, mandatory fields, quality checks).  
- Audit logs for administrative and publishing actions.

5) Analytics and Reporting  
- Article views, search queries, and search success metrics.  
- Feedback signals and deflection indicators (reduced ticket creation after article views).  
- Dashboards for top content, outdated pages, and high-impact gaps.  
- Exportable reports for stakeholders and operational reviews.

Target Users and Stakeholders  
- Customers/End Users: Search and consume help content to resolve issues independently.  
- Support Agents: Reference articles to resolve cases faster and ensure consistent answers.  
- Product Teams: Publish release notes, known issues, and product guidance.  
- Content Managers/Editors: Maintain quality, accuracy, and structure of the knowledge base.  
- Administrators: Configure access, integrations, and system settings.

Assumptions and Constraints  
- Content owners are available to produce and maintain articles with defined SLAs for updates.  
- The portal must integrate with existing authentication solutions for internal users if required.  
- Search performance and uptime are critical due to customer-facing nature.  
- Content must follow organizational compliance requirements (privacy, security, branding).

Success Criteria  
- Increased self-service resolution rates and reduced repetitive support tickets.  
- High search success rate (users finding relevant articles within a small number of queries).  
- Reduced average time-to-resolution for support agents using consistent reference material.  
- Improved content freshness with regular review cycles and measurable update compliance.  
- Positive user feedback trends and reduced “article not helpful” responses over time.

Deliverables  
- Public-facing knowledge base portal with core browsing and search functionality.  
- Administrative interface for content creation, review, publishing, and user management.  
- Documentation including content standards, workflows, and operational runbooks.  
- Analytics dashboards and reports aligned with support and product stakeholders.

High-Level Timeline (Illustrative)  
- Discovery and requirements: Define users, content taxonomy, workflows, and integrations.  
- Design: UX/UI, information architecture, accessibility review, and content templates.  
- Build: Implement portal, CMS functionality, search, roles, and workflows.  
- Test: Functional testing, security review, performance validation, and content QA.  
- Launch: Production release, training for contributors/editors, and measurement setup.  
- Iterate: Continuous improvement based on analytics, feedback, and new product needs.
